Agent-Based Perpetual Futures Market Simulator

Overview

A research framework for simulating perpetual futures markets, including exchange mechanics, order book dynamics, market makers, takers, funding rates, leveraged positions, and liquidation events.

Research Goals:

Study market microstructure dynamics
Evaluate market-making strategies
Analyze liquidity and execution behavior
Explore the interaction between leverage, funding, and liquidations

Current Features:

✅ Limit order book

✅ Matching engine

✅ Market maker agents

✅ Taker agents

✅ Funding rate mechanics

✅ Position tracking

✅ Liquidation engine

✅ Price simulation

✅ Performance metrics

Planned Research:

Inventory-aware quoting strategies

Market maker parameter optimization

Adverse selection analysis

Volatility-dependent spread optimization

Multi-agent strategy comparisons
